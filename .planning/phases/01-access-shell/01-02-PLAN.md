## Phase Goal

**As a** Warwick session friend (MW, JG, JS, or IT), **I want to** sign in with Spotify and reach a private dashboard while the public archive page stays open to anyone, **so that** the four of us can use authenticated tools without locking visitors out of the read-only archive.

> Plan 02 of 4 — the authenticated vertical slice. Adds the OAuth UI on top of Plans 01-01 (scaffold) and 01-01b (auth wiring + proxy.ts + public page): the global header with Sign-in / sign-out controls and the dashboard Server Component that verifies the session and renders the signed-in confirmation. After this plan, a real user can complete the Spotify OAuth flow end-to-end — the Walking Skeleton's headline capability is proven.
>
> Revision iteration 2 note: the original Plan 02 Task 2 (`proxy.ts`) was moved to Plan 01-01b for cohesion — proxy.ts depends on the cookie contract established by `src/lib/auth.ts` in 01-01b, and shipping them together eliminates a cross-plan dependency on the cookie name. Plan 02 now depends on both 01-01 and 01-01b.
